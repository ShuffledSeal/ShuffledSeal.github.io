Æther Observatory

A small window into the infinite.

PLAY THE LIVE EXPERIENCE ↗

AN INVITATION TO WANDER

The sky is serious.
The playground isn’t.

Enter the observatory ↗

Æther is a Victorian-inspired cosmic playground, combining astronomical curiosity with playful, hands-on experiments. Its forest-green palette, brass detailing, and Cormorant typography bring the feeling of an old celestial atlas into an interactive world.

Catch stardust, build impossible planets, develop a photograph, or compose a miniature solar-system soundtrack. Keep the things you make in your own discovery cabinet.

HTML / CSSJavaScriptCanvas / SVGWeb Audio

UNDER THE STARS / UNDER THE HOOD

Handcrafted, all the way down.

Dependency-free HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Fixed-timestep canvas physics, seeded daily runs, custom SVG illustrations, and synthesized audio. Responsive layouts, keyboard controls, and reduced-motion support bring the same care to how it feels to use.
